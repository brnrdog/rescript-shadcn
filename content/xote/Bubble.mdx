## Usage

```res
<Bubble.Group>
  <Bubble>
    <Bubble.Content> {"Hey, are we still on for tomorrow?"} </Bubble.Content>
  </Bubble>
  <Bubble variant=Muted align=End>
    <Bubble.Content> {"Yes — 10am works."} </Bubble.Content>
  </Bubble>
</Bubble.Group>
```
