## Usage

```res showLineNumbers
<Card>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title> {"Card Title"} </Card.Title>
    <Card.Description> {"Card Description"} </Card.Description>
    <Card.Action> {"Card Action"} </Card.Action>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Content>
    <p> {"Card Content"} </p>
  </Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer>
    <p> {"Card Footer"} </p>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card>
```

dependency beyond xote itself.
