## Usage

```res

  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title> {"Card Title"} </Card.Title>
    <Card.Description> {"Card Description"} </Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Content> {"Card Content"} </Card.Content>
  <Card.Footer> {"Card Footer"} </Card.Footer>
</Card>
```
