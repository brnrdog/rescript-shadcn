## Usage

```res
let selected = Signal.make(None)

<DatePicker
  selected={MaybeSignal.reactive(selected)}
  onSelect={date => Signal.set(selected, Some(date))}
/>
```

A `Calendar` inside a `Popover`; picking a day closes it.
