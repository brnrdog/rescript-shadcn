## Usage

```res
<Pagination>
  <Pagination.Content>
    <Pagination.Item>
      <Pagination.Previous href="#" />
    </Pagination.Item>
    <Pagination.Item>
      <Pagination.Link href="#" isActive=true> {"1"} </Pagination.Link>
    </Pagination.Item>
    <Pagination.Item>
      <Pagination.Next href="#" />
    </Pagination.Item>
  </Pagination.Content>
</Pagination>
```
