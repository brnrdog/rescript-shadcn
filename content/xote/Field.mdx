## Usage

```res
<Field.Group>
  <Field>
    <Field.Label for_="name"> {"Name"} </Field.Label>
    <Input id="name" placeholder="Evil Rabbit" />
    <Field.Description> {"This appears on your public profile."} </Field.Description>
  </Field>
</Field.Group>
```

`orientation` is `Vertical`, `Horizontal` or `Responsive`; the last switches at
the field group's container breakpoint.

## API Reference

Behaviour, ARIA wiring and keyboard interaction come from [`rescript-xote-base`](https://github.com/miriadhq/rescript-shadcn/tree/main/packages/xote-base); the component in your project owns the markup and the classes.
