## Usage

```res
Sonner.toast(~title="Deployed", ~description="Your changes are live.")->ignore

<Sonner.Toaster />
```

`Sonner` is the same queue and viewport as `Toast` under a sonner-flavoured
name — pick whichever call site reads better; they render together.
