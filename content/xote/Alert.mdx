## Usage

```res showLineNumbers
<Alert>
  <Icons.Info />
  <Alert.Title> {"Heads up!"} </Alert.Title>
  <Alert.Description>
    {"You can add components and dependencies to your app using the cli."}
  </Alert.Description>
</Alert>
```
