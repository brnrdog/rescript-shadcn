## Usage

```res
<InputGroup>
  <InputGroup.Addon>
    <Icons.Search />
  </InputGroup.Addon>
  <InputGroup.Input placeholder="Search..." />
</InputGroup>
```

`align` places an addon at the inline start or end, or on its own block above or
below the control.
