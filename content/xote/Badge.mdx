## Usage

```res showLineNumbers
<Badge variant=Badge.Variant.Outline> {"Badge"} </Badge>
```

## Examples

### Variants

Use the `variant` prop to change the variant of the badge.

<ComponentPreview name="BadgeVariants" />

### With Icon

You can render an icon inside the badge. Use `dataIcon=InlineStart` to render the icon on the left and `dataIcon=InlineEnd` to render the icon on the right.

<ComponentPreview name="BadgeIcon" />

### Custom Colors

You can customize the colors of a badge by adding custom classes such as `bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-800` to the `Badge` component.

<ComponentPreview name="BadgeColors" />
