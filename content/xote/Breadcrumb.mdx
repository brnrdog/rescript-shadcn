## Usage

```res
<Breadcrumb>
  <Breadcrumb.List>
    <Breadcrumb.Item>
      <Breadcrumb.Link href="/"> {"Home"} </Breadcrumb.Link>
    </Breadcrumb.Item>
    <Breadcrumb.Separator />
    <Breadcrumb.Item>
      <Breadcrumb.Page> {"Components"} </Breadcrumb.Page>
    </Breadcrumb.Item>
  </Breadcrumb.List>
</Breadcrumb>
```
