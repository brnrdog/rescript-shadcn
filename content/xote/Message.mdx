## Usage

```res
<Message>
  <Message.Avatar>
    <Avatar size=Sm>
      <Avatar.Fallback> {"CN"} </Avatar.Fallback>
    </Avatar>
  </Message.Avatar>
  <Message.Content>
    <Message.Header> {"shadcn"} </Message.Header>
    <Bubble>
      <Bubble.Content> {"The registry build is green."} </Bubble.Content>
    </Bubble>
  </Message.Content>
</Message>
```
